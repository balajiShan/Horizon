      Where do I put my interests and topics?
      
Not in this file. This file controls where posts come from (sources), which AI model runs
      (ai), the score cut-offs (processing) and the digest layout. What counts as interesting is
      decided by the profiles: profiles/<name>/analysis.md is the scoring rubric the AI follows, and
      match.md says what content belongs to that profile. To score for your own niche, edit or add a profile, then
      point your sources at it with "profile": "<name>".

      
Every save keeps a timestamped backup in data/config-backups/ (the last 20). Changes apply to your next
      fetch or score; nothing needs restarting.
